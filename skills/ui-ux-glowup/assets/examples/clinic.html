What we treat

If your problem is not listed, call us: we will tell you honestly whether physiotherapy can help.

  • Back and neck pain

    Stiffness, sciatica, pain from desk work or lifting. We find what loads the spine and build a plan you can follow at home.

  • Sports injuries

    Sprains, tendon and muscle problems, knee and shoulder pain. A return-to-sport plan with clear milestones.

  • After surgery

    Rehabilitation following knee, hip and shoulder operations, in line with your surgeon's protocol.

  • Joint pain and arthritis

    Exercises and advice to keep moving with less pain, and to know what to avoid.

What happens at your first visit
