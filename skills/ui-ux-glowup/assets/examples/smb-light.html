Questions before you start.

Four answers owners ask for first. Anything else: hello@example.com.

Do my crews need to install anything?+

No. Crews open a link on their phone and see today's jobs. The app for iPhone and Android is optional and adds offline access.

Can I import my clients from a spreadsheet?+

Yes. Upload a CSV or Excel file with names, addresses and phone numbers. Fieldbook shows the rows it could not read before anything is saved.

Does it work with my accounting software?+

Invoices export to QuickBooks Online and Xero, or as a CSV file for any other tool. Payments recorded in Fieldbook are included in the export.

What happens at the end of the trial?+

The workspace becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted and no card is charged until you choose a plan.
