Frequently asked questions

Another question? Write to us — we reply within 4 business hours.

How are members counted?+

Only members active during the month are billed. Guests (clients, freelancers) are free and unlimited on the Team plan.

Can I switch plans mid-month?+

Yes. Upgrades are prorated to the day; downgrades take effect on the next invoice.

Where is our data hosted?+

In Europe (Frankfurt) by default. The Enterprise plan lets you choose the region and get a signed DPA.

Do you offer nonprofit and education pricing?+

Yes, 50% off the Team plan for nonprofits, schools and open-source projects.
