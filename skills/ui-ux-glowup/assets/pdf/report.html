Key findings

The call button is hidden on phones

It appears after 1,900 px of scrolling; 64% of mobile visitors leave before reaching it.

01

Mobile experience

On a phone, the first screen shows a stock photo and a 60-word welcome paragraph. The phone number is in the footer only, and the menu button is 28 px wide — below the 44 px people can tap reliably.

Visitors who do reach the contact form meet eight required fields, including "company name" and "how did you hear about us". Half of the started forms are abandoned at those two fields.

What to change

Put a sticky "Call now" bar at the bottom of the screen on phones, move the three services and the reviews above the fold, and cut the form to name, phone and a short message.

03

Local search

The site ranks on page 3 for "emergency plumber Leeds" (412 searches a month). The title tag, the Google Business profile and two service pages still name Bradford, where the company was based until 2024.

There is no page per service: boiler repair, leak repair and bathroom installation share one long page, so Google can't match any of them to a specific search.

What to change

Update the title to "Emergency plumber in Leeds, 24/7 — Hollis Plumbing", create one page per service with its own photos and prices, add LocalBusiness structured data, and ask the last 30 customers for a Google review.
